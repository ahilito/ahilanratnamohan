Newsletter #2 - October 2026


Greetings from Floridsdorf 1210, that's the football club I've been working at since the middle of August.  Tomorrow, Dominik Tchatchoua aka Dominik the Regista and Moritz Kern aka First Touch Moritz are gonna lead their first ever Klapping Initiation to launch the new season at Tanzquartier.

That's what I meant to send you 2 weeks ago.  Before I got lost in the hype of the final prep.  Dominik the Regista and First Touch Moritz ended up killing it.  Some photos here.


I got back to Antwerp and straight into All Day I Dream About Soccer (ADIDAS), which we will premiere at Kaaistudio's in Brussels before touring to a bunch of places in Belgium, the Netherlands and Germany (full list below).  


In the background Pidgin-Antwerpen is still simmering and working towards presentation as part of Red Star Line Museum's exposition, Language and Migration (dates below).


I think I wrote about all this stuff in the last newsletter.  So now actual news:  at the end of the year I decided to try and bring some structure into my chaotic practice and to apply for structural funding from the Flemish Government.  For the last few months, together with Enrica Camporesi and David Elchardus (and a whole bunch of people behind the scenes), I've been forming a company.  More on that when we're a bit more ready.


Also with Rekto:verso, we launched an edition which is very close to my heart.  Simpel (Simple) was an edition of our somewhat high-brow cultural critique magazine, in which all articles were written in clear/simple/plain* language.  The edition is a bit of a reaction to the advice every newcomer gets when first learning the language, 'read lots of kids books' and an attempt at wrestling with the exclusionary language of a lot of arts discourse.  My own contribution was about Yotam Ottolenghi and his recipe book Simple.


*all of these words are quite contested, no disrespect intended

Newsletter #1 - July 2026


Dear friend or colleague,


After years of not really communicating about my projects so consistently and slipping behind on social media, I've decided to give a mailing list a try.  

If you're interested in following my artistic work/projects then you can subscribe here.


It will go a little along the lines of the following:

------


This summer I'll be in Vienna working on Klapping the fictional, football-derived streetdance, which I founded with Feras Shaheen (aka Feras the Shield) back in 2018, which ended up becoming real - in a sort of co-residency at Football Club 1210 and Tanzquartier.  If you've got any Vienna friends, we're doing Initiations on the 19th and 20th of September, but also a bunch of workshops in the lead up.  


After that it's straight back into the studio to work on All Day I Dream About Soccer (ADIDAS), a football-dance solo, which started during a residency at Ballhaus Ost in Berlin last June.  I'm looking into my obsession with fashion in football, reminiscing about the jerseys of all the teams I played against in U7's and looking at the recuperation of football fashion through the big brands and labels these days.  It premieres at Kaai in Brussels on October the 8th and then goes on tour through Belgium, Germany and the Netherlands [dates below]


In between all of this I'm attempting to germinate karuvapilai (curry leaf plants) in my living room, partly for my own cooking needs, and partly for the piece Invasive Language, which I started writing at the end of 2022, but presented last November at Utp as a work-in-progress at their Another World Festival.  We'll be working on that again in July


Boiling away in the background is Pidgin Antwerpen, together with Enrica Camporesi and a bunch of fellow Dutch-as-second-language speakers in Antwerp we're creating a new Pidgin Dutch, based on the words people miss from their mother tongue. From the 20th of November until the 11th of April 2027, the new language will be part of the exhibition Language & Migration at Red Star Line Museum in collaboration with Kunst In Zicht.

We share a bit of the process on 5 December through a public 'Language Board'. 


Also, I've been on the editorial team of the culture and critique magazine, Rekto:verso, since August '25.  Here are a couple of articles which I've written during that period: Gepassioneerde Pedagogie [Dutch], Work-in-progress [Dutch], Down Kurukkel Lane [English & Dutch].
